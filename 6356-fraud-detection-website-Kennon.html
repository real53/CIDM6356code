Financial Fraud Detection Model

The calculations below demonstrate findings from "The model of fraud detection in financial statements by means of financial ratios" (Kanapickienė & Grundienė, 2015). This study examined 165 financial statements from Lithuania (40 fraudulent, 125 non-fraudulent) and, using logistic regression, derived a formula predictive of fraud based on four key financial ratios. Model 3 was considered the best after statistical evaluation. The total percentage of the model’s correctly classified cases was 84.8%.

y= 1.936*(Cash/Current Liabilities) +4.766+(Total Liabilities/Total Assets) +0.029*(Sales/Fixed Assets) +4.263*(Inventory/Total Assets) -5.768

Care should be taken in applying this analysis to financial statements from other countries.
